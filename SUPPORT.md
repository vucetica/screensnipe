# Getting Support

Thanks for using ScreenSnipe. Here's where to go depending on what you need.

## Bug reports & feature requests

Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/vucetica/screensnipe/issues/new/choose) using one of the templates. Please search existing issues first.

## Questions, ideas, general discussion

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/vucetica/screensnipe/discussions). Discussions are a better fit than issues for "how do I…", "what do you think about…", or anything open-ended.

## Security vulnerabilities

**Do not** open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting process.

## App Store purchase or installation issues

Email **support@screensnipe.app**. This is the right channel for refunds, billing, Family Sharing, and anything else the Mac App Store handles directly.

## Other private support

Email **support@screensnipe.app**. Replies usually go out within a few days.

## What's not supported

ScreenSnipe is a free indie macOS app maintained in spare time. There's no SLA, and not every feature request will be accepted — but everything gets read.
